## Protocol

Training seeds: 42, 43, 44. Each policy/condition uses 200 episodes with environment seeds 10000–10199. Conditions are random friction 0.3–0.9 and fixed 0.3, 0.6, 0.9, 1.0. PPO uncertainty is across three training seeds; baseline Wilson intervals describe episode sampling uncertainty.

PPO: 13,695 stable successes and 1,305 time limits. Baselines: 806 stable successes and 1,194 time limits. All 17,000 episodes completed; this does **not** mean all episodes succeeded.

## Interpretation notes

Historical reports and audit records are preserved unchanged. Any wording implying all baseline episodes succeeded is incorrect. Hybrid has higher stable-success rates than PI in all five evaluated conditions; this does not imply superiority on every metric. Entering the target without stable success does not by itself establish residual sliding as the cause. PPO comparisons refer to three-seed means unless individual seeds are explicitly identified. Net XY contact force is a proxy, not a complete safety measure.
